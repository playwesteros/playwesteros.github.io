# Westeros: version history

Updated whenever a major change ships. Newest first.

## v1.4.5 (Nov 13, 2026)
- **"Have you played before?"** Asked once, straight after your name. The answer is remembered.
- **The tutorial is a real practice game.** Choosing *This is my first time* seats you in a round against bots on the actual game screen — the real top bar, the real three menus, the real forms and the real war map. Actions are resolved on your own device, so nothing touches the game you joined, and the real game carries on untouched behind it.
- **You play it, you don't click through it.** Pressing Money opens the Money menu, because it is the Money menu. You pick the house and type the amount yourself to send a gift, offer a public or secret alliance, answer the King's decree, recruit and watch upkeep climb, stake gold on spies, and march on a city you choose off the map. Each step moves on by itself the moment you do the thing.
- **The spotlight shades, it does not black out.** The rest of the screen stays readable behind a light wash while the step's control is ringed in gold.
- **Leaving puts you straight back** into your own game, wherever it has got to.
- **Reopen it any time** from *How to play*, under the sigil in the top left.

## v1.4.4 (Nov 11, 2026)
- **Bigger city pieces:** castles are larger on the map, with the name and house label moved clear of them.
- **One troop count per city:** the three troop chips are replaced by a single total in the bottom right of your city piece, like every other piece. Hover it for the breakdown; tap it to start sending troops.

## v1.4.3 (Nov 11, 2026)
- **Unchosen houses are gone from the map:** capitals of houses nobody picked are no longer drawn.
- **Your own cities stay solid** while you pick a war target (they used to fade like the ones you cannot attack).
- **Army counts sit by the city:** troops at home show as small count chips beside your city piece instead of separate pieces. Tap a chip to start sending that kind of force.
- **Tap armies to change orders:** tap a marching army on the map. If it has not left yet, its order is withdrawn and the war panel opens with the same target and amount ready to change; if it is already on the road you can call it back home.

## v1.4.2 (Nov 11, 2026)
- **City markers match the unit pieces:** each capital is the castle icon recoloured to its owner's colour (grey if nobody chose that house) standing on the same chess-piece base.
- **No attacking houses nobody chose:** capitals of houses with no active player can no longer be targeted, on the map and on the server.
- **Subtler march paths:** thinner, fainter lines and a smaller landing beach for armies and fleets.

## v1.4.1 (Nov 11, 2026)
- **Ship routes on the map:** ships now sail through open water only, kept off the shoreline, and land on the beach nearest their target. Choosing Ships and a target city previews the planned route (with a ringed landing beach); ships on the march travel along it. Rounds of travel still come from the server.
- **Chess-piece troops:** on the map, infantry, ships and dragons use the game's own unit icons, recoloured to your house colour (cream halo and dark outline so they stand out on land and sea) and standing on a chess-piece base. Your troops at home show one piece per type with its count; marches show the piece with a count badge. The army table and battle dice keep their original icons.

## v1.2.7 (Nov 10, 2026)
- **Hosting right after a refresh now works.** The green light only shows once the server actually answers; a dead first connection is reset automatically, and pressing Host while it connects waits and then opens the game (with retries) instead of doing nothing.
- **Banners never move.** Removed the remaining pulse and hover scaling on map banners, and the drop-in motion on announcement banners (they now just fade).

## v1.2.5 (Nov 9, 2026)
- **Whisper filter reworked.** With several players selected you see everything each of them sent you, even if it was also sent to others. Your own whispers show only if you sent them to exactly the selected group. Selecting everyone shows every secret message sent to you.

## v1.2.4 (Nov 7, 2026)
- **No repeat notifications in the ledger.** Alliance offers and treaty offers no longer appear in the ledger, since they already have their own menus and badges. Red NEW marks in the ledger are now only for things that have no popup or menu of their own (gifts received). Decrees, treaties, alliances and sieges keep their popups and menus.

## v1.2.3 (Nov 7, 2026)
- **No more stale files after an update.** The stylesheet and scripts are now loaded using the game version number, so every version bump makes browsers fetch fresh copies automatically. The version is set in one place in index.html.

## v1.2.2 (Nov 7, 2026)
- **Banner fix actually reaches browsers.** The page kept loading the old cached stylesheet, so the banners still hopped. The stylesheet and script links now carry new version numbers so browsers fetch the current files.

## v1.2.1 (Nov 7, 2026)
- **Secret messages follow your selection.** Pick one or more players to whisper to and only the whispers shared with exactly those players are shown. Pick nobody to see them all.
- **Map banners stay still** (the hovering pop-out on hover/tap is unchanged).
- **Separate land and sea attacks.** The war form has a Soldiers / Ships / Dragons selector above the number wheel. Each attack sends one kind of force (the server enforces it, and bots send mixed forces as separate armies). Dragons only appear as an option for House Targaryen.

## v1.2.0 (Nov 7, 2026)
- **Cleaner ledger.** Entries are grouped by round, with an icon, a coloured edge (green good, red bad) and the amount on the right. Filter buttons show only Money, Soldiers, Battles, Spies, Diplomacy or Cities.
- **It now records events as well as coins and soldiers:** gold and troops in and out (income, upkeep, gifts, treaties, decrees, recruiting, disbanding, spy spending, plunder, deserters, armies sent and returned, losses defending); spies you sent (success, failure, caught) and spies of others you caught; battles (sieges, field battles, victories, defeats, attacks on you, sieges of your cities); cities that fell or were sacked; alliances made and broken, alliance offers and treaties offered to you, royal decrees, the crown changing hands, traitor labels, other notices.
- **Red marks for things with no popup.** Gifts, treaties, alliance offers, decree answers and plunder you receive show as red NEW rows, with a red count on the filter, the Ledger heading, the Reports button and the Money menu until you close the menu.

## v1.1.9 (Nov 7, 2026)
- **Bots understand what a message refers to.** If a bot king sent you a tax decree and you say "I will pay soon", it knows you mean the tax: it notes when to expect the payment and reminds you (and holds it against you) if the time passes. The same goes for "I cannot afford it", "give me more time" and "I will not pay". If the king asks you to "pay me" or "where is my tax", a bot recognises it as the decree it hasn't settled and pays, repeats its answer, or says it is coming.
- **Bots say when they do not understand.** Instead of random filler, a bot now says it doesn't follow, and mentions any house name it picked up.
- **Bots stop answering replies.** Messages like "ok", "very well", "sounds good" or "your welcome" almost never get an answer. A thank-you right after a bot has agreed to help still gets a "you are welcome" most of the time.

## v1.1.8 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Map:** capital names are hidden unless you hover (or tap on a touch screen). Banners now hop up and down, and each city shows the castle icon on a disc in its owner's colour instead of a dot.
- **Dice:** no more glowing. Each die is tinted with its house colour, shows a clear unit icon (infantry, ship, dragon or garrison) and the house banner, so allied armies fighting together are easy to tell apart.

## v1.1.7 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Send as many armies as you like.** You can now send reinforcements to the same city back to back, or several attacks in the same round. Each army marches on its own.
- **Call an army back.** A "Call back" button lists every army on the road. If it has not left yet it comes home at once; otherwise it turns around and takes as long to return as it has already marched.
- **Armies fight on the road.** When two enemy armies cross paths on the way to different cities, they battle where they meet. There is no defender, so tied dice are discarded. The fight repeats until one army is destroyed and the survivor marches on. Allies never fight each other on the road. The dice replay works for these battles too.

## v1.1.6 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Bots understand who is doing what.** "I will send you 5 gold" is now an offer (they thank you), not a request for money. Messages are read clause by clause, so several things in one message are all understood.
- **Caught spies.** Tell a bot you caught its spies and it admits, denies or defies you, depending on whether its spies really were caught and on its personality.
- **Specific threats.** Threatening to attack, label them a traitor (carries weight only from the king), break an alliance or expose them each get a different answer.
- **Ultimatums.** "If you don't give me 5 gold, I'm going to attack you" is weighed against your strength and their pride: they may pay under protest, stall, or defy and turn hostile.
- Announcing an attack on someone else gets a reaction based on whether that house is their ally or enemy.

## v1.1.5 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Bots no longer fake loyalty to an attacker.** Once a house marches on a bot's city (or its spies see the army coming), the bot treats that house as an enemy for several rounds: it refuses alliances, treaties, gifts, help and requests to follow, answers coldly, and stops starting friendly conversations with them.
- **Bots no longer ask the king what they think of the king.** Questions about the crown are skipped for the king, and talk about the crown is addressed to them directly.

## v1.1.4 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Secret messages to bots.** Bots now appear in the private-message recipient list, so you can message them secretly (and include them under "Everyone").

## v1.1.3 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **More natural bots.** Bots now talk like people instead of repeating house words: they answer greetings, "how are you", questions about their plans (honestly or not), "why", the king, insults and trust talk, with a warmer or colder tone depending on how they feel about you. House mottos are rare now.
- **They answer and ask.** Bots answer anything sent to them, join in on about half of public chat (one bot per message), and sometimes ask questions back or start a conversation, about your plans, who you worry about, the king, or alliances. Your answers can feed into what they think.
- **Royal decrees.** A bot that gets a demand from the king may pay (often with a note), say it will pay later (and then do, once it can afford it), say it cannot afford it, plead poverty falsely, or openly defy the crown, in private or sometimes in public. Defiance costs the king's trust.

## v1.1.2 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Ships sail through water.** Fleets now follow the real coastline: the shortest route through open sea between two cities, plus a short march if a city is inland. Ships travel at 1200 map units per round (infantry 300, dragons 700), so they beat armies on most trips, though sailing around the whole continent can still take a round or two.
- **Sand-blue sea.** The war map's water is now a sand blue instead of parchment, and the internal region borders are clipped to the land.
- **Watch the dice.** Every siege battle's dice animation now plays for the whole table, not just the houses involved, and the Battles list in the reports page has a "Watch the dice" button to replay recent battles.

## v1.1.1 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Smarter bots.** Bots now understand plain-language requests in chat: ask for gold, troops, an attack on a house or city, help, peace, their spy intel, or to follow you. They decide using trust, alliances, their stance toward you, the king's crown and a random buffer, and they can say yes, say no, or lie.
- **Spying and intel.** Bots send spies every round, read the reports (army counts, marches, secret pacts, intercepted ravens) and share valuable finds with allies. They can also hold back or lie to help themselves, and catch lies that contradict their own spies.
- **Reactions.** Bots respond when their spies are caught, when branded a traitor, when an ally breaks a pact, when attacked or besieged (they call allies for help), and warm to gifts. A bot king brands traitors by grudge.
- **Plans and loyalty.** Each round a bot picks a goal (build, conquer, defend, support, betray, hold) and, for every house, decides whether following or betraying pays better, with a randomized buffer. A bot betraying an ally breaks the alliance before attacking. Bots also ask allies for gold, troops, help and joint attacks.
- **Attack fixes.** Bots only attack cities that are live this round (held by a player still in the game, or the empty throne), they estimate their odds with the siege dice rules, keep a home guard, and no longer cap recruiting at 10. Fixed the home-city check bug.

## v1.1.0 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Siege mechanics.** Attacks now lay siege to a city instead of resolving in one roll. Every court, each unit on both sides rolls a die (dragons add +3). Each side's dice are lined up from highest to lowest and compared in pairs: the higher roll wins and the loser's unit is lost, ties go to the defenders, and dice with no opponent are discarded. The siege goes on, round after round, until one side has no units left.
- Defenders are the city's garrison (its defense value, plus the +2 house buffs), the owner's home army, and any relief armies. Surviving attackers and relief armies march home when the siege ends.
- **No income while besieged.** A city pays nothing to anyone until the siege ends or is lifted.
- **Relief armies.** Allies (or teammates) can send an army to a besieged city and add their dice to the defenders. Attackers can withdraw at any time during the action phase.
- **Dice animation.** Each battle plays out as a popup: the dice tumble, sort from highest to lowest, then clash pair by pair, with a casualty summary at the end.
- The map marks besieged cities, and the war panel shows your armies at sieges.

## v1.0.9 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Travel takes time based on distance.** An attack's travel time now comes from the straight-line map distance between the attacker's city and the target. Infantry cover 300 map units per round, ships 450, dragons 700, rounded up to whole rounds. A mixed force arrives with its slowest unit. The attack form shows the travel time before you commit.
- **House buffs on the house-choice screen.** The buff appears under the house's motto when you hover or preview a house (hidden when the host turns buffs off).
- In-game screen fits the window with no page scrolling. The bottom Leave game and War map buttons are gone (Leave game is in the throne menu, the war map is in Army).

## v1.0.8 (Nov 6, 2026)
- **Ready buttons.** Players press Ready in the top bar; the host's button shows the ready count. New host setting: host must wait for everyone, host may move on early with a confirmation (default), or no ready buttons.
- **No more resolution page.** Ending court goes straight into the next round, and every player gets a start-of-round report popup: income, upkeep, ships completed, deserters, and treasury change.
- Recruit as many units as you can afford (the quantity wheel stops at what you can afford). Spy gold wheel stops at 20 or what you can afford. Dragons can no longer be disbanded.
- Changing your name mid-game now takes effect after the game ends.
- Game type comes before player limit on the host's setup screen.
